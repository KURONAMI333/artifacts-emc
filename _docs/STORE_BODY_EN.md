Give exploration-found Artifacts EMC with ProjectE, so found items can enter the transmutation economy. Included examples are Flippers, Snowshoes, Cloud in a Bottle, and Umbrella. Toggle states keep matching EMC values. Loot, recipes, and item effects are unchanged.

This is an independent, data-only ProjectE addon for [Artifacts by Wouter Pauwels (ochotonida)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/artifacts), whose [original source is MIT-licensed](https://github.com/ochotonida/artifacts/blob/4b67278df94e4d3669119f1abb24a1c3a758afe1/LICENSE). Artifacts itself adds wearable items and their effects; this addon only adds KURONAMI-authored EMC conversion data. It does not bundle Artifacts or ProjectE code, replace the original mod, or change its loot, recipes, or effects. The original mods are required separately. [This addon's complete source and portable builders](https://github.com/KURONAMI333/artifacts-emc) are public.

Supported versions:

*   Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge: Artifacts 13.2.5 and ProjectE 1.1.0; 40 item IDs and 45 state records.
*   Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge: Artifacts 9.5.19 and ProjectE PE1.0.1; 36 item IDs and 40 state records.

Progression-sensitive items, Everlasting Beef, Eternal Steak, and the mimic spawn egg are excluded. Prices are authored by this addon, rather than pre-existing ProjectE values.

The NeoForge isolated server confirmed all 45 state records with ProjectE Integration and Recipe Integration present. Forge client checks covered representative toggle values, actual toggling, save/reopen, and reload. All 40 Forge tooltip identities were not individually remeasured; learned-item and condenser behavior and extra modpack overrides are not guaranteed.
